## Information this site collects directly

None. There is no sign-up, commenting, or contact form, and this site's server code does not store visitor information. The hosting provider may keep access logs (IP address, browser information, request time) for a short period for security and operations.

## Ads and cookies

This site shows ads through Google AdSense.

- Third-party advertisers, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this site and other sites.
- Google can use advertising cookies to show personalized ads based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
- You can turn off personalized ads in [Google Ads Settings](https://www.google.com/settings/ads). You can also turn off other advertisers' personalized ad cookies at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).
- How Google uses data is described in the [Google Partner Sites policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, a consent dialog appears before any advertising cookies are used.

## Browser storage

Your screen theme choice (light or dark) is saved in your browser's local storage. This value stays in your browser and is never sent to a server.
